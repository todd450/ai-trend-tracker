How this is measured

Track record

For the 1-week to 6-month views, the page replays the past year week by week with today’s rules and works out what it would have called. A call is counted once, in the week it first appears: a topic called rising eight weeks running is one call. It held if, 13 weeks later, the share had moved further the way it was called. This is a replay of the past year with today’s rules and today’s counts, not a record of what the page said at the time, and the counts can have been revised since.

The 1-year view needs a year of weeks to make a call and 13 more to check it, and the store holds two years, so it has no record yet; the 2-year view cannot have one. Rest of the method as today.
