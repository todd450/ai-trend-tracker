What was said

Day one was about running AI agents in bulk rather than one at a time. Speakers described “software factories”: many agents writing, checking and merging code, with people setting the rules and deciding how far to trust them. A second theme was small, fast “decision” models that answer yes-or-no questions in a fraction of a second, used beside the large models to cut cost and to check agents’ work. Day two turned to what an organisation has to change to absorb that much code: disposable test environments, tests that exist before the code does, review that keeps pace, and people who still decide what is worth building.
